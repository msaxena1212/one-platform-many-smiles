Lease Management Process
1. Unit Reservation – Lease Module
When a prospective tenant expresses interest in a unit, the Marketing Agent shall reserve the selected unit in the Lease Module.
The reservation should include:
Property and unit details
Prospective tenant name
Marketing Agent details
Proposed lease period
Expected lease start date
Agreed or proposed rental amount
Reservation validity period
Any special conditions or remarks
Once reserved, the unit status shall be changed from Available to Reserved to prevent it from being offered to another client during the reservation period.
If the reservation is not converted into a lease within the approved validity period, the system should automatically notify the concerned Marketing Agent and Leasing Department for extension, cancellation, or release of the unit.
2. Customer Creation – Customer Master
When the client confirms their interest in proceeding with the lease, the Marketing Agent shall forward the client’s details and documents to the Leasing Department.
The Leasing Department shall verify the submitted information and create the tenant profile in the Customer Master.
The tenant information may include:
Full name or company name
Qatar ID, passport, or Commercial Registration details
Nationality
Mobile number and email address
Permanent and local address
Authorized signatory details, where applicable
Emergency contact details
Employer or company information
Uploaded supporting documents
Document issue and expiry dates
The system should prevent duplicate customer creation using identifiers such as Qatar ID, passport number, Commercial Registration number, mobile number, or email address.
3. Document Verification and Approval
The Leasing Department shall review and verify all mandatory tenant documents before preparing the Lease Agreement.
The required documents may include:
Qatar ID
Passport copy
Residence permit
Company Commercial Registration and Computer Card, where applicable
Authorized signatory documents
Salary certificate or employment confirmation, if required
Security deposit and payment details
Any landlord-specific documents
The document verification status should be recorded as:
Pending
Verified
Rejected
Additional Information Required
The Lease Agreement should not be generated until all mandatory documents are verified or an authorized approval is provided.
4. Lease Agreement Creation – Lease Module
After successful document verification, the Leasing Department shall create the Lease Agreement in the Lease Module.
The Lease Agreement should include:
Tenant details
Property and unit details
Lease commencement and expiry dates
Rental amount and payment frequency
Security deposit
Number and value of post-dated cheques
Grace period, penalties, and renewal terms
Maintenance responsibilities
Utility responsibilities
Parking or additional facility details
Special conditions
Notice period for renewal or termination
The system should generate the lease payment schedule based on the agreed rental frequency, such as monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.
The Lease Agreement shall first be provided to the tenant for review and signature.
5. Tenant Signature
The tenant shall review and sign the Lease Agreement either physically or electronically.
The signed copy should be uploaded and attached to the lease record.
The system should record:
Tenant signature date
Signed document attachment
Name of the person who received the signed agreement
Pending landlord signature status
At this stage, the lease status may be updated to Tenant Signed – Pending Collection and Landlord Signature.
6. Collection of Rent, PDCs and Security Deposit – Finance Module
Based on the approved Lease Agreement and payment schedule, the tenant shall submit the required payments to the Cashier.
The collection may include:
Advance rent
Post-dated cheques
Security deposit
Agency commission
Registration or administrative charges
Utility deposit
Other approved charges
The Cashier shall update all received payments and cheque details in the Finance Module against the relevant Lease Agreement.
For each post-dated cheque, the system should capture:
Cheque number
Bank name
Cheque date
Cheque amount
Tenant or payer name
Lease reference
Payment period
Cheque status
Cheque statuses may include:
Received
Deposited
Cleared
Returned
Replaced
Cancelled
The security deposit should be recorded separately from rental income and linked to the tenant and lease.
7. Receipt Generation – Finance Module
Once payment, security deposit, or post-dated cheques are received, the Cashier shall generate the applicable collection receipts from the Finance Module.
Separate receipts may be generated for:
Rent collection
Security deposit
Agency commission
Administrative charges
Other collections
Each receipt should contain:
Receipt number and date
Tenant name
Property and unit
Lease reference
Payment type
Payment method
Cheque or transaction reference
Amount collected
Collection period
Cashier details
The receipt should be available for printing, emailing, or sharing with the tenant.
8. Submission for Landlord Signature
After the tenant has signed the Lease Agreement and the required collections have been completed, the Leasing Department shall submit the following documents to the landlord or authorized signatory:
Tenant-signed Lease Agreement
Collection receipts
Post-dated cheque summary
Security deposit receipt
Tenant supporting documents
Any required approval documents
The landlord or authorized person shall review and sign the Lease Agreement.
The fully signed Lease Agreement should be uploaded to the Lease Module and shared with the tenant.
The lease status shall then be updated to Active, subject to completion of the key handover and check-in process.
9. Key Issue Notification
After completion of the payment collection and signatures from both the tenant and landlord, the Leasing Department shall generate a Key Issue Notification.
The notification should be sent to:
Tenant
Property Manager
Concerned property staff
Security team, where applicable
Maintenance or facility team, where applicable
The notification should include:
Tenant name
Property and unit details
Lease commencement date
Approved key handover date and time
Number and type of keys or access cards
Name of the authorized person collecting the keys
Outstanding requirements, if any
Property Manager or staff contact details
No key should be issued unless the lease is approved, required collections are completed, and the landlord or authorized person has signed the Lease Agreement.
10. Key Handover
The tenant shall collect the keys, access cards, parking cards, or other property access items from the concerned Property Manager or authorized staff.
The Key Handover Form should record:
Key handover date and time
Number and type of keys issued
Access cards issued
Parking remote or access device details
Meter or utility information
Tenant acknowledgement
Name and signature of the issuing staff
The tenant and the concerned staff shall acknowledge the handover.
11. Check-In Process
During the check-in process, the tenant and Property Manager or authorized staff shall inspect the unit.
The Check-In Report may include:
Unit condition
Furniture and appliance condition
Fixtures and fittings
Wall, floor, ceiling, door, and window condition
Electricity meter reading
Water meter reading
Air-conditioning condition
Number of keys and access cards
Photographs or videos of the unit
Existing damages or observations
Pending maintenance work
The tenant shall review and acknowledge the Check-In Report.
Any existing damage or pending maintenance issue should be recorded and assigned to the concerned team for resolution.
Once the tenant acknowledges the documents, the check-in process shall be marked as completed and the unit status shall be updated to Occupied.
12. Lease Renewal Notification
The system should generate an automatic lease expiry and renewal notification at least two months before the Lease Agreement expiry date.
Notifications should be sent to:
Tenant
Leasing Department
Marketing Agent, where applicable
Property Manager
Landlord or authorized person, where required
The renewal notification should include:
Current lease expiry date
Proposed renewal period
Proposed rental amount
Revised terms, if any
Required notice period
Last date for renewal confirmation
Outstanding payments or obligations
The Leasing Department shall follow up with the tenant and update the renewal status as:
Renewal Confirmed
Renewal Under Discussion
Non-Renewal Confirmed
Awaiting Tenant Response
13. Lease Renewal Process
If the tenant confirms renewal, the Leasing Department shall initiate the Lease Renewal process in the Lease Module.
The renewal process should include:
Review of tenant documents and expiry dates
Update of rental amount and lease terms
Renewal approval, where applicable
Creation of the renewed Lease Agreement
Tenant signature
Collection of new post-dated cheques or payments
Adjustment or continuation of the security deposit
Generation of collection receipts
Landlord or authorized person’s signature
Activation of the renewed lease period
The renewed lease should remain linked to the previous lease for complete tenant and unit history.
14. Non-Renewal and Check-Out Process
If the tenant confirms non-renewal, the Leasing Department shall initiate the Check-Out process.
The system should record:
Tenant’s non-renewal notice
Planned move-out date
Notice submission date
Outstanding rent or charges
Required inspection date
Utility clearance requirements
Key return requirements
Before check-out, the Finance Department should confirm whether there are any outstanding balances.
The Property Manager or authorized staff shall conduct a final unit inspection and prepare the Check-Out Report.
The report should compare the unit condition against the original Check-In Report and identify:
Normal wear and tear
Tenant-caused damages
Missing items
Maintenance or repair charges
Utility balances
Unreturned keys or access cards
Cleaning or restoration charges
The tenant shall acknowledge the Check-Out Report and return all keys, access cards, remotes, and other property items.
15. Security Deposit Settlement and Lease Closure
After completion of the check-out inspection, the applicable charges shall be calculated and adjusted against the security deposit.
The Finance Department shall prepare the final tenant settlement showing:
Security deposit received
Outstanding rent
Maintenance or damage charges
Utility charges
Other deductions
Refundable balance
Any security deposit refund should be processed after obtaining the required approval.
Once the final settlement, refund, document handover, and key return are completed, the lease shall be marked as Closed.
The unit status should then be updated to:
Vacant – Available
Vacant – Under Maintenance
Vacant – Reserved
The complete lease history, documents, receipts, inspection reports, and settlement details should remain available in the system for future reference and audit purposes.
This can also be converted into an SRS workflow with responsible department, module, input, approval, system status, and output for each stage.
